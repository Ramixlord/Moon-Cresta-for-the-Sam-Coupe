SCREENSStraight from the SAM, in SimCoupé.

THE WHOLE ARCADE GAMEFrom the moment the disk boots to the high-score table.

THE THREE-STAGE ROCKET

Lose a stage and the survivors regroup. Each stage fires on its own, so two or three shots can be in the air at once.

DOCKING

The next stage rises from below and you bring your ship down onto it against gravity and drift before the 30-second clock runs out. Seconds left × 100, × 200 or × 300, depending on which stages join.

FAR OUT

Clear the last wave and the rocket blasts off the top of the screen. The game loops harder, and you start again with a single stage.

EXTRA ROCKET

Earned at 30,000 points with its own jingle — and one extra game when you run out.

1 OR 2 PLAYERS

Insert credits from the main menu, pick one or two players, and take turns after each hit.

DEFINE KEYS

Set your own left, right and fire. Cursor keys and the SAM joystick work too.

FULL ATTRACT MODE

Title, main menu, score advance table, best 5 pilots and the “Trip to the Space War” story page, typed out a line a second.

HIGH SCORES

Enter your initials. The top scorer's initials take the place of the Nichibutsu logo under HI-SCORE.

BUILT FROM THE ARCADENothing was guessed from videos.

The original Moon Cresta ROM was decoded and run in a purpose-built arcade emulator that logs sprites, sound and memory frame by frame. Every part of the SAM version was checked side by side against it.

ENEMY FLIGHT
The arcade's 28-direction velocity table and 96 flight segments, driven by the same phase machine, so every swoop, dive and loop follows the arcade path.
WAVES & REGROUPS
How each wave enters; the frozen pause after a hit; the spin and shrink into the centre; the Atomic Piles drifting off-screen.
MUSIC & JINGLES
The arcade melody player and all 17 of its tunes, from the stage-start fanfare to a separate kill jingle for each enemy type.
THE SHOT
Modelled on the arcade's own sound circuit: a rough “pssh” that rises to about 3.9 kHz and fades over half a second.
EXPLOSIONS
The arcade's choice for each event — a small burst for kills, a spreading debris ring for meteors, a 32 × 32 blast for the rocket.
FIRING RULES
One stage fires per press: the first of I, II and III whose own shot has finished.
DOCKING PHYSICS
The arcade's throttle model for gravity and drift, the 30:0 clock, the ±7-pixel touchdown window, and the crash and time-out outcomes.
SCORING
Kill values, the docking multipliers, the 30,000-point extra rocket and the extra game.
FAR OUT & THE LOOP
The rocket's climb, the text timing, and the restart with a single stage.

UNDER THE HOODAn engine written for the SAM's 6 MHz Z80.

  • Graphics. Mode 4 with two screen buffers swapped every frame, so nothing tears. Sprites are masked run-length data that skips empty pixels; each buffer keeps its own list of what to erase.
  • Mapping the arcade. The arcade's 224 × 256 portrait screen maps onto the SAM's 256 × 192 landscape by shifting x 16 pixels and scaling y by ¾, so its paths carry over directly.
  • Timing. The SAM runs at 50 frames a second while the arcade logic runs at 30 updates; a fixed-point accumulator paces each piece of logic at its original rate.
  • Sound. The arcade's melody and effect players ported to the SAA1099, with every pitch recalculated for the SAM's chip. The shot mixes tone and noise for the arcade's rough edge.
  • Memory. Game code sits in one 32 KB block; enemy graphics are swapped in from paged banks for each wave.
  • Starfield & text. The stars only draw on empty background, and the CREDIT line and messages are redrawn under the sprites, so ships pass cleanly over them.

HOW IT'S BUILTOne script goes from artwork to a bootable disk in seconds.

  1. GRAPHICSSprites cut from the artwork sheets, plus font, logos and loading screen, packed into the SAM sprite format.
  2. ARCADE DATAFlight tables, spawn points, melodies and effects extracted from the arcade ROM.
  3. ASSEMBLYThe Z80 source is assembled with pyz80.
  4. DISKMasterDOS, a BASIC loader, the intro, the loading screen and the game, written to one 800K disk.
  5. TESTINGEvery build runs in SimCoupé under script control and is compared with the arcade emulator.

PLAY ITReal SAM or SimCoupé.

Download MoonCresta.mgt and boot it. On a real SAM Coupé, write the image to an 800K disk; in SimCoupé, insert it in drive 1 and reset. The disk loads the Lightning Studios intro, then the loading screen, then the game.

Press 4 to insert a credit, choose 1 or 2 players, and press fire.
